| City Weather Forecast | `/v7/weather/now`<br>`/v7/weather/{days}`<br>`/v7/weather/{hours}` | 2027-06-01 | [Link](https://blog.qweather.com/announce/weather-forecast-webapi-v7-discontinue/) |
